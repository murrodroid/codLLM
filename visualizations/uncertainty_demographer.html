The full tradeoff

As you flag a larger share of the model's least-confident predictions for review, the auto-coded remainder gets cleaner. The curve is steep early — the first 1% you flag catches 41% of all errors — and flattens fast. Past the 25% mark there is nothing left for a coder to find.

codLLM · Validated on 13,828 records held out of training "Confidence" here is the model's own assessment of how likely its prediction is. The lowest-confidence cases concentrate the errors: the bottom 10% by confidence contains 99% of all the model's mistakes.
