Who We Are

A bachelor-thesis project at the Technical University of Denmark, built in collaboration with practising historical demographers.

The team

  • Bachelor students at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU)
  • Working on this as our thesis project
  • Spring cohort, 2026

The origin

  • Project initiated thanks to Mads Perner & Tim Riswick
  • Built around a real research need in historical demography
  • In dialogue with practising coders along the way

The output

  • Our bachelor thesis, defended at DTU spring 2026
  • A separate research paper for the demography community
  • Open code & reproducibility for the experiments
